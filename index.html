Featured SEO Case Study 01

Scaling Organic Growth for a Kenya-Based Solar Energy Website

Agency project — Client details anonymized to respect confidentiality agreements.

IndustrySolar Energy & Power Solutions
Target MarketKenya
Project DurationApproximately 5–6 Months
My RoleSEO Executive
Core FocusTechnical, On-Page, Content & Off-Page SEO

🚩 The Challenge

The Kenya-based solar energy website had very limited organic visibility at the beginning of the engagement. The project required a complete SEO improvement process across core service pages, product pages, supporting blogs, technical foundations, and off-page authority in a competitive solar-energy market.

Primary Challenges:

  • Very low initial organic traffic and search visibility.
  • Core and product pages required stronger keyword targeting.
  • Technical SEO issues needed to be documented and resolved.
  • Supporting blog content had to be planned around relevant search demand.
  • Strong competition across solar and renewable-energy searches in Kenya.
  • Need to improve both informational discovery and commercial search visibility.

🚀 SEO Growth Strategy

I worked on the website’s core pages, product pages, blog content, technical SEO recommendations, and link-building activities to establish stronger relevance, crawlability, keyword coverage, and authority.

Core Workstreams:
  • Keyword research and keyword targeting for service and product pages.
  • On-page optimization of titles, meta descriptions, headings, content, URLs, and keyword placement.
  • Blog planning, optimization, and publishing to expand organic keyword coverage.
  • Prepared a technical SEO blueprint and shared implementation recommendations with the development team.
  • Improved internal linking and page-level relevance across the website.
  • Executed relevant link-building and selected guest-posting activities to support authority growth.

Execution Phases

Phase 1 — Website & Market Analysis

Reviewed the website, solar-energy product offerings, target market, competitors, existing page structure, and initial organic performance.

Phase 2 — Core & Product Page Optimization

Optimized important service and product pages using relevant keywords, improved metadata, headings, content structure, internal linking, and search-intent alignment.

Phase 3 — Technical SEO Blueprint

Audited technical SEO elements and prepared a detailed blueprint covering crawlability, indexing, redirects, canonical signals, sitemap, robots.txt, page performance, and other required fixes for the development team.

Phase 4 — Blog Content & Organic Expansion

Planned, optimized, and uploaded supporting blog content targeting informational and problem-solving queries related to solar products and energy solutions.

Phase 5 — Authority & Link Building

Worked on relevant link-building activities, including selected guest posts and external placements, to strengthen the website’s authority and support target-page visibility.

Phase 6 — Monitoring & Iterative Optimization

Monitored Google Search Console and GA4 performance, reviewed clicks, impressions, organic users, keyword movement, and page-level performance to guide ongoing optimization.

📈 Results & Business Impact

Organic Traffic Growth

Scaled monthly organic traffic from a near-zero baseline — under 100 organic users — to approximately 9,000 monthly organic users within around five to six months.

Search Visibility

Achieved substantial growth in Google Search Console visibility, with impressions and clicks increasing by approximately 3x–4x during the optimization period.

Page-Level Growth

Improved the organic performance of core service pages, product pages, and supporting blog content through targeted on-page and content optimization.

Technical & Authority Improvements

Created and communicated a technical SEO blueprint for issue resolution while supporting website authority through relevant link-building and guest-posting activities.

Featured SEO Case Study 02

SEO Foundation & Website Planning for a Quick-Commerce Construction Materials Website

Agency project — Client details anonymized to respect confidentiality agreements.

IndustryQuick-Commerce Construction Materials
Project StageInitial SEO Foundation
Primary Scope10 Main Categories + 55+ Subcategories
My RoleDigital Marketing Analyst
Core FocusKeyword Research, Mapping & SEO Architecture

🚩 The Project Requirement

The business was preparing to launch a quick-commerce website for construction materials such as wires, fans, electrical products, and other small construction-related items. The website needed a clear SEO structure before development and content implementation.

Key Requirements:

  • Build a comprehensive keyword database for the complete website.
  • Identify primary and secondary keywords for every category and subcategory.
  • Map search intent and keywords to the correct target URLs.
  • Create a scalable website hierarchy for approximately 10 main categories and 55+ subcategories.
  • Ensure SEO requirements were considered before website development.

🚀 SEO Planning Approach

My responsibility was to establish the SEO foundation from the beginning so the website could be developed with proper keyword targeting, URL structure, category hierarchy, and search-intent alignment.

Core Deliverables:
  • Detailed keyword research and master keyword file.
  • Primary and secondary keyword identification for all pages.
  • Keyword-to-URL mapping across categories and subcategories.
  • SEO-friendly URL recommendations and page naming structure.
  • Website hierarchy and on-site SEO recommendations.
  • SEO implementation SOP and development recommendations.

Project Execution Phases

Phase 1 — Business & Website Understanding

Reviewed the business model, product range, target customers, planned website categories, and the way users would search for construction materials online.

Phase 2 — Comprehensive Keyword Research

Conducted keyword research for the main product categories and subcategories, identifying relevant commercial, transactional, and product-focused search terms.

Phase 3 — Keyword Master File & Mapping

Prepared and organized the keyword master file, assigning primary and secondary keywords to all 10 major categories and 55+ subcategories.

Phase 4 — URL & Website Structure Planning

Recommended SEO-friendly URLs, category naming conventions, page hierarchy, and a scalable structure to support crawlability, relevance, and future expansion.

Phase 5 — On-Site SEO Recommendations

Prepared recommendations covering metadata, headings, content placement, image optimization, banner requirements, crawlability, indexing, Core Web Vitals, and other essential on-site SEO considerations.

Phase 6 — SEO SOP & Development Handoff

Created a detailed SEO implementation document/SOP and shared the recommendations with the development and client teams to ensure SEO was incorporated from the website-building stage.

📌 Project Deliverables & Impact

Keyword Coverage

Created a structured keyword master file covering the website’s major categories and subcategories.

SEO Site Architecture

Mapped keywords to relevant URLs and planned a scalable category and subcategory hierarchy.

Development Readiness

Delivered SEO recommendations and an implementation SOP so the website could be developed with SEO requirements in place from the beginning.

End-to-End SEO Growth for an Interior Design Business

Agency project — Client details anonymized to respect confidentiality agreements.

IndustryInterior Design & Home Interiors
Project ScopeEnd-to-End SEO Execution
Target MarketBangalore
My RoleSEO Executive
Core FocusTechnical, On-Page, Local & Content SEO

🚩 The Challenge

The interior design website was only partially optimized. The project required a complete SEO process to understand the business goals, identify relevant service searches, improve website targeting, and generate qualified enquiries from users searching for interior design services in and around Bangalore.

Primary Challenges:

  • Limited optimization across important service pages.
  • Unclear keyword targeting for major interior design services.
  • Need for stronger content and search-intent alignment.
  • Technical and on-page SEO improvements across the website.
  • Need to improve visibility for local and commercial searches.
  • Limited organic visibility and lead-generation performance.

🚀 End-to-End SEO Strategy

I handled the project from initial research and planning through implementation, monitoring, and continuous optimization, with a focus on improving relevant organic visibility and converting search demand into enquiries.

Core Workstreams:
  • Business understanding, competitor research, and keyword strategy.
  • Keyword mapping for service pages and supporting content.
  • On-page optimization and search-intent-focused content improvements.
  • Technical SEO fixes and structured data implementation.
  • Internal linking, local SEO, and Google Business Profile optimization.
  • Performance tracking through Google Search Console, GA4, and Microsoft Clarity.

Execution Phases

Phase 1 — Business & Competitor Research

Understood the service offerings, target audience, locations, competitors, and search demand to establish a practical SEO roadmap.

Phase 2 — Keyword Research & Mapping

Researched and mapped commercial and informational keywords for modular kitchens, living room interiors, hospitality interiors, corporate interiors, and other relevant services.

Phase 3 — Technical SEO Improvements

Reviewed and improved crawlability, indexing, canonical signals, redirects, sitemap and robots.txt considerations, structured data, and other technical SEO elements.

Phase 4 — On-Page & Content Optimization

Optimized page titles, meta descriptions, headings, URLs, content structure, keyword placement, image elements, and service-page relevance based on search intent.

Phase 5 — Internal Linking & Local SEO

Strengthened internal linking between relevant pages, improved local search signals, and optimized Google Business Profile elements for relevant local searches.

Phase 6 — Monitoring & Continuous Optimization

Tracked clicks, impressions, CTR, rankings, organic traffic, user behaviour, and lead-related activity using Google Search Console, GA4, and Microsoft Clarity.

📈 Results & Business Impact

Organic Traffic Growth

Achieved a strong improvement in organic traffic, with performance increasing by approximately 2x–3x during the optimization period.

Keyword Visibility

Improved rankings for multiple high-intent service keywords, with several important terms moving from lower search-result pages to Google Page 1.

Lead Generation

Improved organic visibility contributed to more relevant lead-form submissions and qualified enquiries, with SEO performance increasingly supporting the business development pipeline.

Blog-Driven Discovery

Planned and optimized supporting blog content to capture informational search demand, expand keyword coverage, and create additional entry points into the service pages.

Featured SEO Case Study 01

Improving Organic Visibility for an Interior Design Company

Client: Confidential (Interior Design Company) — Client information has been anonymized to respect confidentiality agreements.

IndustryInterior Design
Duration6 Months
CMS PlatformWordPress
My RoleSEO Executive
Core FocusTechnical, On-Page, Local & Content

🚩 The Challenge

The client wanted to improve search visibility for its core service pages while also generating organic traffic through informative blog content.

Primary Challenges:

  • Important service pages were not ranking competitively.
  • Blog articles lacked search visibility.
  • On-page SEO inconsistencies across multiple pages.
  • Weak internal linking between services and blogs.
  • Limited keyword coverage for high-intent searches.
  • Technical issues affecting crawlability and indexation.

🚀 SEO Strategy

Instead of focusing only on rankings, I developed a structured SEO strategy to strengthen both the website's technical foundation and topical relevance.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Comprehensive Technical SEO & Crawl Audits
  • Advanced On-Page Tag & Structure Refinement
  • Commercial & Informational Keyword Mapping
  • Internal Architecture & Silo Linking Frameworks
  • E-E-A-T Focused Content Enhancements
  • Rigorous Rank & Performance Tracking via GSC/GA4

Execution Phases

Phase 1 — Technical Audit

Analyzed crawlability, indexing, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical verification, broken links, Core Web Vitals, and mobile usability.

Phase 2 — On-Page Optimization

Optimized Meta Titles, Descriptions, heading hierarchy, URL structures, image ALT tags, content enrichment, and search intent alignment.

Phase 3 — Keyword Research

Executed competitor analysis, keyword mapping, long-tail opportunity discovery, blog topic research, and search intent categorization.

Phase 4 — Site Architecture

Built a robust internal linking framework connecting services to blogs, optimizing anchor text, and engineering clean search engine crawl paths.

Phase 5 — Content Optimization

Refined commercial service pages and educational blogs for freshness, readability, precise keyword placement, and E-E-A-T signals.

Phase 6 — Monitoring & Tools

Tracked keyword rankings, clicks, impressions, CTR, and indexed pages using Google Search Console, GA4, SEMrush, WordPress, and VS Code.

📈 Proven Results & Impact

Core Service Pages

Successfully ranked multiple high-intent service keywords within Google's Top 10 search results.

Blog Performance

Several optimized blog articles achieved Top 5 Google rankings for targeted informational keywords.

Overall Improvements

Enhanced topical relevance, pristine index health, and a balanced presence across commercial and informational queries.

Featured SEO Case Study 02

Scaling Organic Growth for an International Service Website

Client: Confidential (Service Business – East Africa) — Client information has been anonymized to respect confidentiality agreements.

IndustryService Business
Duration8 Months
Target MarketKenya
My RoleSEO Executive
Core FocusTechnical SEO, On-Page & Strategy

🚩 The Challenge

When the project started, the website had very limited organic visibility and struggled to attract qualified traffic.

Key Obstacles:

  • Low organic traffic (averaging less than 100 monthly users).
  • Weak keyword rankings and suppressed visibility.
  • Poor crawlability, missing structured data, and thin page optimization.
  • Limited internal linking and sparse topical authority.

🚀 The SEO Approach

Instead of immediately chasing vanity metrics, I deployed a disciplined, multi-phase technical and structural workflow.

Core Milestones:
  • Complete Technical Audit & Priority Fixes
  • Advanced Schema & XML Sitemap Architecture
  • Intent-Driven On-Page Page Optimizations
  • Pillar & Cluster Topic Planning
  • Strategic Off-Page Citation & Backlink Acquisition
  • Continuous Data Tracking via GA4 & GSC

Execution Phases

Phase 1 — Website Audit

Evaluated crawlability, indexing, structure, Core Web Vitals, and content gaps to prioritize high-impact execution items.

Phase 2 — Technical SEO

Fixed crawl errors, optimized robots.txt, sitemaps, canonicals, implemented schema markup, resolved duplicates, and optimized URLs.

Phase 3 — On-Page Optimization

Refined Meta Titles, Descriptions, heading trees, keyword densities, search intent alignment, and image assets.

Phase 4 — Keyword Research

Executed competitor analysis, keyword mapping, topic clustering, and long-tail opportunity discovery tailored to the Kenyan market.

Phase 5 — Architecture & Linking

Planned site hierarchy, optimized anchor text distribution, and built a cohesive pillar-and-cluster internal linking framework.

Phase 6 — Authority Building

Conducted backlink opportunity research, competitive gap analysis, citation building, and strategic authority acquisition.

Phase 7 — Performance Monitoring

Continuously measured organic traffic, rankings, impressions, CTR, index coverage, and technical health using GSC, GA4, and SEMrush.

📈 Proven Results & Business Impact

Organic Traffic Growth < 100 → ~9,000 Monthly Users (Peak Oct 2025)
Search Visibility

Multiple high-intent target keywords successfully reached Google Page 1.

Technical Health

Maximized crawl efficiency, clean index coverage, and robust topical authority.
